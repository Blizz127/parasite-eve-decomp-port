* Build r5. Fixes the problems reported on r4b. Every fix below is checked
  against the retail game (matched code, the emulator oracle or the retail
  video).
* New since r4b — please check:
  - Movies now play at full speed, fullscreen too (they were slow on the
    Legion Go). This build is also optimised; earlier builds were not.
  - Saving over an existing save works (Yes replaces it, No keeps it); in
    r4b the game closed at that prompt.
  - Cheat warp menu: a warp picked on the title screen or during a movie is
    refused or waits instead of freezing the game. Use warps while walking
    around in the field.
  - If the game is closed from Steam or crashes, the log now says so.
  - The alligator boss's fire-breath beam and embers now draw (second
    phase), as in the real game.
  - More of the game runs the exact decompiled code.
* Known issues to ignore (being fixed):
  - Using some equipment/tool items from the field menu may still stop the
    game (a fix is being verified). Healing items are fine.
  - Loading screens are shorter than on a real PlayStation (being modelled).
  - Aya's gun model may be missing when she readies it.
  - The date doesn't walk to the door with Aya in the plaza.
  - Full list: docs/port/KNOWN_DIVERGENCES.md (21 open).
* Saves: memory-card saves persist (card file:
  ~/.local/share/parasite-eve-port/memcard1.mcd).
* Cheats (off unless pressed): F1 help, F2 god mode, F3 ammo/PE, F4 win
  battle, F5 key items, F6 fast-forward, F7 FF speed, F8 warp. "CHEATS" shows
  on screen when any is on.
* Bug reports: the time and room, a screenshot, and the newest log file in
  ~/.local/state/parasite-eve-port/logs/ (written automatically).
